# RAVE Photo Editor - Google Play listing

## Store listing (Main store listing)
App name (max 30): RAVE Photo Editor
Short description (max 80): Remove & blur backgrounds with on-device AI. Enhance photos in one tap.

Full description (max 4000):
RAVE Photo Editor brings AI photo editing to your phone, without uploading your photos anywhere.

Pick a photo and:
• AI Cutout - remove the background in one tap and save a transparent PNG for stickers, product shots and profile pictures.
• Portrait - blur the background behind your subject for a soft, lens-like look (light, medium or strong).
• Backdrop - swap the background for a clean colour: white, black, slate, sky, blush, mint, sun or violet.
• Enhance - balance exposure, contrast and colour automatically.
• Filters - Vivid, Warm, Cool, Fade, Mono, Noir and Sepia, with live previews.
• Adjust - fine-tune brightness, contrast, saturation and warmth.
• Compare - press and hold the photo to see the original.
• Save to your gallery or share straight to your favourite apps.

Private by design
The AI that finds your subject runs on your device through Google Play services. Your photos are never uploaded by the app, and there is no account, no ads and no tracking.

Open photos from the app, or share a photo to RAVE Photo Editor from your gallery.
