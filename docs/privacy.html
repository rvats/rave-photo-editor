Privacy Policy

RAVE Photo Editor · RAVE Management LLC · Effective 18 October 2026

RAVE Photo Editor ("the app") is published by RAVE Management LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. In short: your photos are edited on your phone and are never uploaded by the app.

Your photos

  • You choose photos with Android's system photo picker, or by sharing a photo to the app. The app can only see the photos you pick.
  • All editing, including the AI features (background removal, portrait blur and background replacement), runs on your device. The app does not send your photos to us or to anyone else.
  • We do not collect, store or have access to your photos. Photos you save are written to the Pictures/RAVE Photo Editor folder on your device. Photos you share go only to the app or person you choose in Android's share sheet.

On-device AI provided by Google

To find the subject of a photo, the app uses Google's ML Kit subject segmentation, which Google Play services downloads and runs on your device. As described in Google's ML Kit data disclosure, ML Kit may send Google limited diagnostic and usage information, such as device model and operating-system version, the app's package name and version, performance metrics, the feature's configuration, error codes and per-installation identifiers. Google uses this information to maintain and improve ML Kit; it is encrypted in transit. Google's handling of this information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in, and no collection of your name, email address, contacts or location.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking added by us, and no sale or sharing of personal information.

Permissions

The app does not ask for permission to read your photo library, camera, microphone, contacts or location. Saving to your gallery uses Android's standard media storage, which needs no extra permission on Android 10 and later.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

Because we do not collect your photos or personal information, we hold nothing to delete. Edited photos stay on your device until you delete them. Uninstalling the app removes its temporary files.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.
